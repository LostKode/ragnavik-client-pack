Tip: Use the Build Camera to get a better perspective on your constructions. Toggle it on and off with the hotkey B to place objects more precisely.
Tip: Collect epic loot from chests and enemies for powerful gear. Use the enchantment table to further enhance your equipment.
Tip: Secure your base with wards. Access the ward menu by interacting with the ward stone and manage permissions for other players.
Tip: Expand your farming by planting a wider range of plants and trees. Use the hoe to prepare the ground and plant seeds from your inventory.
Tip: Transport items and creatures through portals. Use the teleportation menu to select and move objects across your world.
Tip: Plan your builds before construction. Use the plan hammer to design structures and place ghost versions of items to visualize your projects.
